## Visual language

- Background: near-black / graphite
- Proof accent: green → teal
- PROVEN: green
- PARTIAL: amber
- FAILED: red
- Typography: clean sans-serif + monospace for CLI/output

Keep visuals simple, technical and evidence-oriented. Avoid generic “AI magic” imagery: the identity of Proof is verification, traceability and release confidence.
